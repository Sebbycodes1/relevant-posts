Hard score caps

  • Rumor: maximum 59 and excluded from breaking-event coverage
  • No primary evidence or independent confirmation: maximum 69
  • Commentary without a new underlying event: maximum 74
  • Speculative analysis without primary evidence: maximum 79
  • 90+ requires primary evidence, independent confirmation and a measurable first-order impact
  • The post is scored—not the fame or credibility of the author
  • Verified major events remain visible even when their honest score is below the display threshold

Automatic penalties

  • Recycled news without new information
  • Unsupported certainty or anonymous claims without corroboration
  • Promotion without technical or commercial substance
  • Engagement bait, vagueposting and personality drama
  • Duplicate coverage of the same underlying event

Human feedback loop

Use Save, Useful and Dismiss to tune the queue as you review it. Feedback stays on this device.

PM evidence discipline

  • “Primary evidence” means the item itself supplies first-party data, specifications or documentation
  • “Independently corroborated” requires a separate source and a direct supporting link
  • Repeated coverage is clustered, but repetition alone does not count as confirmation
  • Explicit-name tags aid routing; they are not automated trade recommendations
